Campus Dining Guidelines & Etiquette

  • Tray Return: Please return your empty plates and cutlery to the designated cleaning conveyor belt after dining.
  • Food Waste Reduction: Order only what you can consume to support our campus green sustainability initiative.
  • Token Calling: Keep your order tracking screen open or listen for counter display announcements.
  • Outside Food: Outside commercial packaged food is discouraged inside the main dining hall during peak lunch hours.
  • Hygiene Standard: Kitchen chefs and food servers adhere to mandatory chef caps, gloves, and daily health screenings.
